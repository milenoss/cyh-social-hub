# Choose Your Hard marketing design

Status: approved and implemented, 24 September 2026.

## Direction

An old explorer’s notebook on warm cream: editorial, calm, honest, and tactile. The website uses `#faf2ec` parchment, `#1b1917` warm ink, white paper surfaces, whisper-light lavender rules, and a single accessible blue action colour (`#3659cc`).

DM Serif Display gives the marketing headlines their journal voice. Satoshi handles navigation, body copy, and controls. Inside the phone preview, Bricolage Grotesque, DM Sans, Archivo, the official `#201e1d` / `#ec3013` Hibar mark, and the mobile app’s own UI language keep the product faithful to the iPhone app.

## Rules

- Serif is for large editorial headings, never small interface copy.
- One filled blue action per visual section; other actions are text or outlined.
- Cards use 12px radii, buttons 16px, and borders before shadows.
- The iPhone-style preview is the product hero and its four outcomes are interactive.
- Product claims and sample data come from `/Users/mk1883776/personal/choose-your-hard`.
- No analytics, cookies, sign-in, fake App Store badge, leaderboards, or public pressure language.
- Legal pages use the same visual system and prioritise readable policy text. Keep the pre-release labels until the final-publication gates pass.
