About the service

Choose Your Hard is an accountability app for recording personal challenges, participating in crews, and keeping a private history of your work.

Eligibility and accounts

The current product is intended for people aged 16 or older. The final policy must confirm the launch territories and minors approach.

Use the service safely

The app is not a healthcare provider or emergency service. Members remain responsible for choosing activities appropriate for them and adapting or stopping unsafe activity.

Private by default

Daily check-ins begin private. Members choose when supported content is shared with a crew. Public challenges expose the handle and result while notes and proof remain private.

Information the app uses

The service needs account, challenge, check-in, crew, subscription, and optional notification information. Optional AI requests and shared-content moderation are separate purposes.

Your choices

Members can control sharing, notifications, country participation, and whether to use optional AI features. The app does not need continuous location or a contacts address book for its core flow.

Deleting information

Account deletion removes account-linked data and media through the in-app process, subject to the final retention policy and limited shared challenge information needed by remaining members.

Delete your account

The primary route is immediate deletion inside the app. This page explains the steps and provides support when someone cannot access the app.

Deleting the app does not delete your account or cancel an Apple subscription. Manage the subscription separately through Apple.
  1. Open Choose Your Hard
    Sign in and open the Me tab.
  2. Find Help & Legal
    Select “Delete account immediately”.
  3. Review the warning
    Your private data, media, AI history, and medals will be permanently removed.
  4. Confirm deletion
    The app asks once more before starting the irreversible process.

If you cannot access the app, email support from the address connected to the account. Support may need proportionate information to verify the request.
